against the paper.

Topic: weekly return reversal.

Reported finding: contrarian portfolios that buy the previous week's losers and
sell the previous week's winners earned positive returns in the following week.

Mechanism discussed: overreaction, with bid-ask bounce and trading costs as
competing explanations that reduce the economic size of the effect.

Caveats for reuse: weekly US equities; the reversal is sensitive to costs.
