title: Evidence of Predictable Behavior of Security Returns (Jegadeesh, Journal of Finance, 1990)

HAND-WRITTEN FIXTURE NOTE. A short paraphrase written for this repository's
offline demo; it is not text from the paper and its claims were not re-checked
against the paper.

Topic: short-term reversal in stock returns.

Reported finding: individual stock returns show negative first-order serial
correlation at a one-month horizon, so last month's losers tend to outperform
last month's winners in the following month.

Mechanism discussed: overreaction and liquidity effects at short horizons.

Caveats for reuse: monthly US equities; transaction costs matter for any
strategy built on short-horizon reversal.
