HAND-WRITTEN FIXTURE NOTE. A short paraphrase written for this repository's
offline demo; it is not text from the paper and its claims were not re-checked
against the paper.

Topic: time-series momentum across futures markets.

Reported finding: an instrument's own past 12-month excess return predicts its
future return, across equity index, currency, commodity and bond futures.

Mechanism discussed: initial under-reaction followed by delayed over-reaction.

Caveats for reuse: this is time-series, not cross-sectional, momentum; ranking
assets against each other tests a different claim.
