title: Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers (Jegadeesh and Titman, Journal of Finance, 1993)

HAND-WRITTEN FIXTURE NOTE. A short paraphrase written for this repository's
offline demo; it is not text from the paper and its claims were not re-checked
against the paper.

Topic: cross-sectional momentum in stock returns.

Reported finding: on US stocks, portfolios that buy past winners and sell past
losers, ranked on returns over the previous 3 to 12 months, earned positive
returns over the following 3 to 12 months.

Mechanism discussed: delayed price reaction to firm-specific information.

Caveats for reuse: monthly horizons, US equities, and a skip between ranking and
holding periods; a daily, small-universe lagged-return signal is a loose analogue.
