# Chapter 11. The dangers of backtesting, item by item

v2 of docs/afml_sections/ch11.md: every warning of the chapter's six headings as its own item, each with a test or a
mechanical check that would catch us committing it, or deferred or not applicable with a reason. The chapter is where
this project is most exposed, so three items stay open and say so: the walk-forward's in-sample tuning (F11.1), the
probability of backtest overfitting measured over strategy families rather than over the daily selection (F11.2), and
the single-market universe (F11.3). Registered code is frozen by confirmation v2 until the analysis on 2026-10-24.
