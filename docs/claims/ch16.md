# Chapter 16. Machine learning asset allocation, claim by claim

v2 of `docs/afml_sections/ch16.md`: every logic step, formula, data structure, algorithm and warning of each numbered
heading, and of the four appendices that close the chapter, as its own item. Book text paraphrased throughout.

Hierarchical risk parity clusters assets by a correlation distance, reorders the covariance matrix so similar assets
sit together, and splits capital top-down in inverse proportion to cluster variance, inverting nothing.
`pmlab.afml.hrp` reproduces the book's worked example to four decimals, its numerical example's allocations, and the
ordering of its Monte Carlo. Here the assets are strategies: once they graduate, `pmlab.afml.lifecycle.allocate` splits
capital across them with HRP times a ramp and a health factor (`research/lifecycle.md`).
