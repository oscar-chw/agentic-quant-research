# Chapter 17. Structural breaks, claim by claim

v2 of docs/afml_sections/ch17.md: every logic step, formula, data structure, algorithm, warning and hard constraint of
the chapter's fifteen headings as its own item, each implemented and evaluated, deferred with a reason, or not
applicable with a reason. The book's text is a private reference; statements paraphrase it and never quote it.

What the evaluation looks like here: every statistic of the chapter is checked twice, once against a series with a
known break (it must find it) and once against a series with none (it must not exceed its size), and every closed form
is checked against the value the book itself gives or against a simulation whose answer is known before it runs. The
chapter's cost model (Table 17.1) is now computed rather than quoted: `pmlab.afml.breaks.sadf_flops` reproduces all
three of the book's E-mini numbers exactly, which also pins the minimum sample length behind them at 1000.
