# Chapter 19, second reading

An auditor who did not write `docs/claims/ch19.md` re-read every line of all twenty-one heading spans, listed the
claims, compared them with the items, and opened every test a `done` item names to ask whether it would fail if the
claim were false (plan/book-v2.md node 18). The counting convention is the one stated in `audit/ch18.md`.

This chapter is the one where nearly every claim has a closed form or a simulation with a known answer, so that is the
standard the reading applied: a test that runs an estimator without pinning it against a value known before it ran is a
mismatch. By that standard the chapter holds up well. The estimators are checked against a Kyle equilibrium computed
from its own coefficients, a Roll process with a known half spread, a three-Poisson mixture with a known probability of
informed trading whose residual bias is identified as the uninformed noise floor, and Brownian bars with a known
volatility. Two defects were found: one claim with no item, and one item whose test asserted everything except the
comparison the claim is about.
