# Chapter 22, second reading

An auditor who did not write `docs/claims/ch22.md` re-read every line of all eighteen heading spans, listed the claims,
compared them with the items, opened every test a `done` item names, and checked each not-applicable row against
docs/hardware.md (plan/book-v2.md node 18). The counting convention is the one stated in `audit/ch18.md`.

This is a guest chapter about a machine this project does not have, so 45 of its 64 items end not applicable and the
honesty of those notes IS the work. Every number in them was re-derived and they hold:

- 20 TB against 14 GB recorded and 0.7 GB a day is 78 years of recording, which is right.
- 62.1 M events replayed in about 11 minutes is the 95 k events a second docs/hardware.md records.
- 0.7 GB a day against 2,867 GB free is 0.024% of the disk, quoted as 0.02%.
- The book's own figures check out too: 603.98 seconds against 3.5 hours is 20.9-fold, quoted as the section's 21; 18
  minutes against 1.5 seconds is 720-fold; 603.98 against 2.58 seconds is 234-fold.
- The once-a-minute component would rest on 5 cycles in a 300-second window, and 183 days cannot resolve an annual
  frequency of 366.

What the reading did find: ten figures that no item named, seven notes whose number was written in a form the quantity
rule cannot read ("1-node machine", "5-minute window", "18 headings"), one calibration list missing two of the
section's seven settings, and four claims with no item at all. All are closed.
