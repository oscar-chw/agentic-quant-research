opened. Twenty new items were added and six rows re-cited. Chapter 4 is almost all formulas, and the
recurring defect is a normalised or directional assertion standing in for a level: the worked example's three

0 < u <= 1 was asserted nowhere in the repository; and no test ever called the time-decay function outside the
interval the book defines it on.
