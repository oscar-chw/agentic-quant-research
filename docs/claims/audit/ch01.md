# Chapter 1, second reading (plan/book-v2.md node 15)

An auditor who did not write `docs/claims/ch01.md` re-read every line of all twenty-four heading spans, listed the
claims each span makes, checked them against the items, and opened every test a `done` item names to ask whether it
would fail if the claim were violated. `claims` counts the distinct logic steps, formulas, structures, algorithms,
warnings and constraints of the span - not its analogies or its history. `items` is the count after the fixes.
Thirty-six new items were added and nine rows re-cited or re-statused; the largest single finding is that the chapter's
sharpest pitfall, an in-sample optimum degrading out of sample, rested on an algebraic identity with no sample split
in it at all.
