# Chapter 15. Understanding strategy risk, claim by claim

v2 of `docs/afml_sections/ch15.md`: every formula, algorithm, structure and warning of each numbered heading as its own
item, implemented and evaluated, deferred, or not applicable with a reason. Book text paraphrased throughout.

The chapter models a strategy as independent bets that pay one of two amounts, which ties the annual Sharpe ratio to
precision, frequency and payouts; inverting the tie gives the precision or frequency a target needs, and the chance the
true precision falls below it is the strategy's risk. A token bought at one price and held to settlement pays exactly
two amounts per share, so the model is exact for a maker quoting a fixed price and approximate for a taker buying at
many prices. `pmlab.afml.strategy_risk` holds the formulas; the lifecycle's graduation rule G4 and decommission rule D2
use them with the book's 0.05 bound (`research/lifecycle.md`).
